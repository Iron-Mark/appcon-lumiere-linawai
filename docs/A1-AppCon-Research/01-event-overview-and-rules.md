# 01 — Event overview and rules: AppCon 2026 "Virtual AI Matsuri"

> Verified against the official site, the organizer's Facebook posts, and the sponsorship primer. Anything not verified is flagged **[UNCERTAIN]**.

## 1. What AppCon is

- AppCon ("An Invitational Application Development Contest") is an annual Philippine app-development competition "that aims to resolve social issues in the Philippines" ([site meta description](https://appcon.otismanila.com/)).
- It was launched in 2018 by **OTis Co., Ltd.** (株式会社OTis), a Japanese company headquartered in Tomakomai, Hokkaido, with a Manila development base; the first awarding was April 2019 at San Beda University ([sponsorship primer, slide 1–3](https://www.canva.com/design/DAG-QGNyPF4/CBOk-pFxBwUieoTgo0rMtA/view); [OTis 2019 press release](https://otis.company/archives/915)).
- The Philippine entity is **OTis Philippines Inc.**, Makati (23/F Tower 6789, Ayala Ave.), whose businesses are advertising, IT/custom software, and **human-resource matching** ([otismanila.com](https://otismanila.com/); [AppCon careers page text](https://appcon.otismanila.com/careers)).
- OTis states the contest's purpose bluntly in Japanese: to find "excellent talent with an entrepreneurial spirit" to register on its talent-referral platform and to find "development partners for offshore development" ([2019 press release](https://otis.company/archives/915)). The 2026 site adds that AppCon is "a crucial showcasing platform, connecting both student and professional developers directly with our local and Japanese industry partners" ([site /about → Overview tab](https://appcon.otismanila.com/about)).

## 4. Rules that shape strategy (official wording)

Eligibility and team ([site](https://appcon.otismanila.com/register)):
- Individual registration; select role(s): Developer, UI/UX, Product Designer; multi-role selection allowed.
- Teams of five with fixed composition (3 dev / 1 UI-UX / 1 product designer), "curated matching to ensure complementary skills".
- Free; BYOD; previous Grand Winners of any edition are not eligible.

Theme ([site /about → Theme](https://appcon.otismanila.com/about)):
- "All projects must center on Artificial Intelligence (AI) as the core technology."
- Team leaders join a lottery draw during the hackathon to select the team's sub-theme from a predefined pool of challenge categories; each team gets a distinct sub-theme.

Judging guidelines ([site /about → Judging](https://appcon.otismanila.com/about)):
- "The participant's product will be uploaded to a GitHub repository provided by the organizer."
- "Source codes are used to evaluate the application from the point of the Technology criteria."
- "The organizer has the right to use the submitted code by applicants for marketing and sponsors."

Judging criteria (100 points) — decoded in [03-judging-criteria-decoded.md](03-judging-criteria-decoded.md):
- Product 35%: Relevance 5 · Impact & Value 10 · UI/UX Design 10 · Maintainability & Sustainability 10
- Technology 30%: Functionality 15 · Technical Innovation 15
- Creativity 20%: Originality 10 · Innovation in Design 10
- Presentation 15%: Clarity & Storytelling 10 · Demo & Delivery 5

## 6. Prize economics

- Grand Winner ₱100,000 for a team of 5 = ₱20,000 each; Best in Pitching ₱10,000. Historically Excellence was ₱50,000 (2023), Best Technology ₱30,000 (2023) and Best Product ₱30,000 (2024) ([PSU news](https://main.psu.edu.ph/psu-it-team-bags-silver-medal-in-natl-appcon-2024/); [AMAES news](https://news.amaes.edu.ph/2024/07/ama-university-conquestadors-emerge.html); [LinkedIn, Coronel](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/axllouis-coronel_thrilled-to-share-a-proud-momentmy-team-activity-7347190751501369344-t399)); whether those tiers exist in 2026 is not announced **[UNCERTAIN]** — the public 2026 prize list names only Grand Winner and Best in Pitching.
- Date caveat: the site's 2022 entry says awarding was "April 2022" with development Dec 2021–Mar 2022, but participants' records place the 2022 edition's build in Aug 2022–Mar 2023 and the award in April 2023 ([02 §7](02-past-winners-analysis.md)) **[UNCERTAIN]**. Since 2022 the edition year is the launch year and awarding happens the following year (2023 → June 2024; 2024 → June 2025).
- The non-cash prize is the real one for many: direct exposure to OTis and its Japanese partners, i.e., job/offshore-partner pipelines ([site purpose](https://appcon.otismanila.com/about)). Pitch accordingly (see [07](07-pitch-and-demo-playbook.md)).
