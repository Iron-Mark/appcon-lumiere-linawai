Observations:
- **Every Grand Winner addressed transport/traffic, jobs, road safety, or waste** — the exact "social problems" OTis named when it founded the contest: "traffic, poverty, inequality, garbage" ([OTis 2019 release](https://otis.company/archives/915)).
- **Three of five had a physical/IoT component** (ReVendo, TransiTech, and TRAVIS's camera pipeline); both 2023 and 2024 Best Technology winners were also hardware (flood IoT; a rescue boat). In a 3-day virtual AI hackathon this route is closed — which removes the previous winners' biggest differentiator and levels the field.
- **Every Grand Winner is a system, not a feature**: multiple user roles and a closed loop (sense → decide → notify/act).
- The "AI" in winners was **applied CV/ML with a metric** (k-NN 84%; YOLO; XGBoost), not chat. No Grand Winner was a chatbot. 2026 is the judges' **first LLM-era cohort** — teams that still show a measured model (accuracy on N cases) will match the judges' existing mental model of "real AI".
- The only judge-feedback phrase on record is **"global impact potential"** — impact framed beyond one campus or one city.

## 2. The other award tiers — what each award seems to reward

Awards existed in every edition: Grand Winner, Excellence Award, Best Product, Best Technology, Most Creative, plus sponsor "special awards" in 2022 (CKC) and 2023 (LIEH) ([winners page](https://appcon.otismanila.com/winners); [AMAES news](https://news.amaes.edu.ph/2024/07/ama-university-conquestadors-emerge.html)). Winners describe them as places: Excellence = "1st Runner Up" / "2nd Place", Best Technology = "3rd Place" in 2024, Best Product = "Top 5" ([PSU news](https://main.psu.edu.ph/psu-it-team-bags-silver-medal-in-natl-appcon-2024/); [LinkedIn, Chico](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hanz-chico_i-am-proud-to-share-that-our-team-antechtors-activity-7357765664322867200-0J8D); [UCC news](https://ucc-caloocan.edu.ph/post/congratulations-team-arko); [LinkedIn, Coronel](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/axllouis-coronel_thrilled-to-share-a-proud-momentmy-team-activity-7347190751501369344-t399)). **Inference:** the five awards are an ordered 1st–5th ranking, with the Best/Most labels attached to places 3–5 by the criterion each team topped — so in 2026 the "Grand Winner" is simply the top total score, and the criteria families (Product / Technology / Creativity) that have existed since 2019 are the same ones now weighted 35/30/20.

## 4. How the 2024 champions worked (the best-documented case)

From the [Corner Magazine feature](https://www.cornermagazineph.com/2025/07/05/grand-winner-of-appcon-2024-competition-transitech-smart-transportation-management-system-project-wins-top-prize/), the team leader's [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jonhzuniga_appcon2024-cybergo-transitech-activity-7345677106397237249-ely7), and the coach's [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jlrnrph_hala-totoo-ba-panalo-tayo-maam-we-did-activity-7346357023657140224-uiQz):
- The idea came from a teammate's own painful commute from Bulacan; the team **pivoted away from an agri-tech idea** after re-evaluating "urgency and impact".
- The leader had **already won an AppCon award in 2023** (Best Technology, Conquestadors) — experience with the judges' expectations compounds; the same AMA coaching staff (Neypes, Dr. Sultana, Engr. Leonen) backed both wins ([AMAES news](https://news.amaes.edu.ph/2024/07/ama-university-conquestadors-emerge.html)).
- Clear role split: lead/DevOps/full-stack, IoT lead, two Flutter devs, two React devs + documentation, AI lead, plus two consultants; they "learned new technologies from scratch".
- They **collected real-world LRT data** to train the crowd model and built five IoT devices.
- The coach describes "sleepless nights, intense disagreements, emotionally fueled programming" and "a win we honestly didn't expect" — conflict is normal; what mattered was finishing.
- Process: 20 weekly progress reports, Top 90 (21 Apr 2025) → Top 20 (7 May) → Pitching Week (12–16 May) → awarding (28 Jun).
- The press narrative ends with a **call to pilot with DOTr / LTFRB / MMDA**; OTis's feedback singled out "global impact potential".

Other documented experiences:
- A 3-person NU Dasmariñas team reached the Top 20 "with limited resources and without a coach" ([LinkedIn, Fabian](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cbrf2002_before-our-college-journey-comes-to-an-end-activity-7351417592454041600-sjsP)).
- Columban College describes the phases as System Development → Judging (finalists) → Pitching, with weekly progress updates via a form ([Columban College news](https://columban.edu.ph/index.php?page=news&slug=columban-college-inc-ccs-teams-dailekto-and-calbi-joins-appcon-2024)).
- REPEL (Excellence 2023) was a 10-person team with two faculty mentors; the same team later won DOST seed funding via an incubator ([PSU news](https://main.psu.edu.ph/psu-it-team-bags-silver-medal-in-natl-appcon-2024/); [PSU news 2](https://main.psu.edu.ph/psu-information-technology-team-receives-450k-funding-after-winning-from-miriam-college-tbi-program/)).
- Winning team sizes ranged from 2 (IAM, 2020) to 10 (REPEL, 2023); modal 4–9, larger for hardware builds.
- **Capstone/thesis reuse was the norm** (ReVendo, ARKO, SoilTrack, Conquestadors, MMARRS, NutriSys, BabyMo) — the months-long format favoured teams with a pre-existing prototype and research base. The 2026 lottery sub-theme neutralises this advantage for everyone.

## 5. Repeat-winning schools and people

University of Caloocan City (UCC) holds 7 awards including two Grand Winners (2022, 2023), supplied 10 of 15 entries in 2022 and claims 7 of the Top 20 in 2024 ([winners page](https://appcon.otismanila.com/winners); [UCC news](https://ucc-caloocan.edu.ph/post/congratulations-team-arko)); AMA University went Best Technology 2023 → Grand 2024 with overlapping people; PUP has 5 awards. Grand Winners came from TIP, STI Meycauayan, UCC ×2 and AMA — never from UP/Ateneo/DLSU — which suggests judges reward **working, deployable, locally grounded systems over pedigree** (inference). In 2026 teams are mixed by the organizer, but expect individuals from these programs — and previous non-Grand awardees, who remain eligible — in the field.

## 7. What this history says about the 2026 judges' taste (inference)

1. They reward **tangible, demonstrable systems that visibly solve a mainstream Filipino problem** in OTis's named domains (traffic, jobs/poverty, waste, plus disasters and health); "impact potential" beyond one campus/city is the phrase they used.
2. They reward **AI that does something specific and measurable** (predict crowd, detect accident, route, verify ID) with a number attached — their reference class is YOLO/XGBoost-style applied ML, not chat.
3. They like **incentive/behaviour design and novel interaction concepts** (ReVendo's points; SnapTrash's snap-to-report) — Most Creative has gone to the concept even when the AI was immature.
4. Best Product goes to **complete, polished, multi-role apps in health/livelihood**; Best Technology to the **hardest working tech**; the awards behave like an ordered ranking.
5. They are **hiring**: maintainable code, clear roles, professional delivery, and the 2026 source-code review all point the same way.

Caveats: judges' names, scoring sheets, and deliberation notes were never published; the site's 2022 dates ("April 2022") conflict with participants' evidence that the 2022 edition was built Aug 2022–Mar 2023 and awarded April 2023 **[UNCERTAIN — treat the site as unreliable on 2022 dates]** ([LinkedIn, Aquino](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kyle-aquino); [TalipaApp repo history](https://github.com/saisarah/TalipaAPP-Backend)).
